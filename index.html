Questions owners ask

Do guards need a special phone?

No. Any smartphone with a browser works.

What if there is no internet at the site?

The clock-in is saved on the phone and sent when the internet comes back.

What happens if I pay late?

You have 7 days. After that the office can still view and export, but cannot change anything until you pay. Guards can still clock in, patrol, ask for leave and send SOS.

Can other companies see my data?

No. Each company's data is kept separate.
